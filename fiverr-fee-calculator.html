How much does Fiverr take?

Fiverr charges sellers a flat 20% fee on every order. The fee also applies to gig extras and to tips. So when a buyer pays for a $50 gig, the seller earns $40. The buyer pays a separate service fee on top of the gig price, which does not come out of your earnings.

After the 20% fee, there can be two more small costs:

  • Withdrawal fee: some withdrawal methods have a small fixed fee per withdrawal. Making fewer, larger withdrawals saves money.
  • Currency conversion: if you receive the money in PKR, INR, AED or another currency, the payment service usually takes a small percentage through its exchange rate.

How much does Fiverr charge buyers?

Buyers pay a separate service fee at checkout. According to Fiverr's Help Center, it is 5.5% of the purchase amount, plus $3.50 on orders under $200. Some countries also add tax such as VAT. The buyer pays this fee, so it does not come out of the seller's earnings.

  • $5 gig: buyer pays about $8.78 (about $3.78 fee), seller receives $4.00
  • $50 gig: buyer pays $56.25 ($6.25 fee), seller receives $40.00
  • $100 gig: buyer pays $109.00 ($9.00 fee), seller receives $80.00
  • $200 gig: buyer pays $211.00 ($11.00 fee), seller receives $160.00

Every payment has its own fee, so a tip or an extra added later carries a separate buyer fee. In some regions Fiverr now shows buyers the "all-in" price (gig price plus fees) while they browse. This does not change what the seller earns.

How to use the calculator

  1. "I know my gig price": enter the price, the number of orders and any withdrawal or conversion costs. The calculator shows what you keep.
  2. "I know what I want to receive": enter the amount you want in your pocket. The calculator shows what you need to charge.
  3. Add an exchange rate and currency code (for example 280 and PKR) to see the result in your own money.

Tips to keep more of your Fiverr earnings

  • Price with the fee in mind. If you want to receive $40, your gig should cost $50.
  • Sell packages and extras. Basic, Standard and Premium packages raise the average order value, so the same work earns more.
  • Withdraw less often. One withdrawal a month costs less than four small ones when there is a fixed fee.
  • Bring repeat clients. Happy buyers come back, and repeat orders take less time to win.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Fiverr take from a $5 gig?

Fiverr keeps 20%, so you earn $4 from a $5 order, before any withdrawal or currency costs.

Does Fiverr take 20% of tips too?

Yes. Fiverr's 20% fee also applies to tips and to gig extras.

How long until I can withdraw my Fiverr earnings?

Earnings are usually cleared about 14 days after an order is completed. Some top sellers get a shorter clearing period.

Is the Fiverr fee the same in Pakistan, India and the UAE?

Yes. The 20% seller fee is the same in every country. What changes is the withdrawal method and the currency conversion cost.

How much does Fiverr charge buyers?

Buyers pay a service fee of 5.5% of the purchase amount, plus $3.50 on orders under $200. For a $50 gig, the buyer pays $56.25 before any tax.

Does the buyer service fee reduce my earnings?

No. The buyer pays the service fee on top of the gig price. As a seller you receive 80% of the gig price, so $40 from a $50 order.

Is this calculator official?

No. It is an independent free tool by FuturMind AI. Fiverr can change its fees, so always check Fiverr's own help pages for the latest numbers.

7 Best Free Sora Alternatives in 2026 (After the Shutdown)

OpenAI's Sora is gone. Here are the best AI video generators you can use instead, with free plans, what each one is good at and how to choose the right one.

Published: October 2, 2026 · By Ghulam Mustafa Janjua

In March 2026, OpenAI announced that it would shut down Sora, its popular AI video generator. The Sora app and website closed on April 26, 2026, and the Sora API for developers ended on September 24, 2026. If you used Sora for YouTube Shorts, Reels or creative projects, you now need a new tool. You can read the full story on our AI News page.

The good news: in 2026 there are several strong AI video generators, and most of them let you start for free. This guide compares the best options for beginners, explains what "free" really means, and helps you pick the right tool for your needs.

1. Kling AI: Best Overall Sora Alternative

Kling AI, made by the Chinese company Kuaishou, is the tool many former Sora users moved to. It creates realistic human movement, keeps characters fairly consistent and supports both text-to-video and image-to-video. Its free plan gives you credits that refresh regularly, which is great for practice.

Good to know: the company is based in China, so read its privacy policy if you plan to upload personal photos or client material.

2. Google Veo: Best Cinematic Quality

Google's Veo models are known for very high quality, cinematic camera work and built-in sound and dialogue. Access is mainly through Google's Gemini app and paid Google AI plans, and free access is limited and can depend on your country. If you already pay for Google's AI plan, Veo is one of the strongest options available.

3. Hailuo (MiniMax): Best for Expressive Motion

Hailuo creates lively, expressive movement and is popular for short viral-style clips. It offers free credits for beginners and fairly cheap paid plans. Like Kling, it is run by a China-based company, so keep the same privacy advice in mind.

4. Pika: Best for Fun Social Media Clips

Pika is simple and beginner-friendly, with creative effects that work well for Reels, TikTok and Shorts. The free plan gives you a monthly allowance of credits. It is a good choice if you want quick, playful clips rather than movie-like scenes.

5. Luma Dream Machine: Best Camera Movement

Luma is known for smooth camera moves and fast generation. It works well for product shots, scenery and short cinematic moments. The free allowance is small, so use it to test prompts before choosing a paid plan.

6. Dreamina (Seedance): Best for Daily Practice

Dreamina is ByteDance's creative platform (the company behind TikTok and CapCut), and it gives access to its Seedance video models. It offers free daily credits, which makes it useful for beginners who want to practice a little every day.

7. Runway: Best for Editing and Professional Work

Runway is more than a video generator. It includes editing tools that help you change, extend and polish clips, which makes it popular with marketing teams and serious creators. The free plan only gives one-time credits, so it is best for testing before you pay. We also cover Runway in our best free AI video generators guide.

How to Choose the Right Sora Alternative

  • Want the most free practice? Start with Kling AI or Dreamina.
  • Want the best quality and have a budget? Try Google Veo.
  • Making fun short clips for social media? Try Pika or Hailuo.
  • Need editing tools for client work? Choose Runway.
  • Product or scenery shots? Try Luma Dream Machine.

Smart tip: Use two or three free plans together. For example, practice daily on Kling or Dreamina, and use another tool for special clips. This way you get more free videos every month.

How to Move Your Workflow from Sora

  1. Save your best old Sora prompts in a document
  2. Test the same prompt in two or three new tools and compare results
  3. Keep your scripts, voiceovers and project files on your own computer
  4. Combine your clips, captions and music in an editor like CapCut

Can You Earn Money with AI Videos?

Yes, but be careful. In July 2026, YouTube clarified that mass-produced, repetitive or template-based videos cannot earn ad revenue. AI clips work best when they support your own story, script, research or commentary. You can also offer AI video editing as a freelance service; see How to Earn Money on Fiverr with AI and our YouTube Shorts with AI guide.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Paying for a yearly plan before testing the free version
  • Using free, watermarked videos for paid client work
  • Creating videos of real people without their permission
  • Copying famous movie characters or brand logos
  • Depending on only one tool for your whole business
  • Uploading private photos without reading the privacy policy

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Sora still available in 2026?

No. The Sora app and website closed on April 26, 2026, and the Sora API ended on September 24, 2026. OpenAI asked users to download their videos before these dates.

What is the best free alternative to Sora?

For most beginners, Kling AI is the best free starting point because its free credits refresh regularly. Dreamina, Hailuo and Pika also offer useful free plans.

Do free AI video tools add a watermark?

Usually yes. Most free plans add a watermark and have limits on video length, quality and commercial use. Paid plans normally remove the watermark.

Which Sora alternative has the best quality?

Google Veo and Kling AI are often rated among the highest quality options in 2026. The best choice depends on your type of video and budget, so test your own prompts.

Can I use these tools on my phone?

Many of these tools have mobile apps or work in a mobile browser. For editing and combining clips, a computer is usually easier.

Final Recommendation

Sora's shutdown is a reminder that AI tools can change quickly. Start with free plans like Kling AI and Dreamina, test the same prompts in a few tools, and only pay when you know which one fits your work. Keep your own files and scripts safe, and focus on creating videos that add real value for your audience.

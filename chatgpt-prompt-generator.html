How to use the prompt generator

  1. Write your task in one sentence. For example, "Write a YouTube script about 5 free AI tools for students". You can also press one of the quick-start buttons and replace the parts in [brackets].
  2. Choose a role. Telling the AI to act as a teacher, copywriter or freelancing expert changes the style and the depth of the answer.
  3. Describe your audience. "Beginners on a phone" and "business owners" need very different answers.
  4. Add details. Your goal, the product, the key points and anything to avoid. More detail usually means a better first answer.
  5. Pick tone, format and length, then press Copy prompt and paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude.

Why a good prompt matters

AI chatbots only know what you tell them. A short prompt such as "write a script" gives a general answer that you then have to fix again and again. A clear prompt explains who the AI should be, what it should do, who it is for and how the answer should look. That saves time, because the first answer is already close to what you need.

This generator follows a simple structure that works in every popular AI tool:

  • Role: who the AI should act as.
  • Task: the exact job.
  • Audience and context: the background the AI cannot guess.
  • Requirements: tone, format, length and language.

Tips for even better answers

  • Keep the conversation going. After the first answer, ask for changes: "make it shorter", "add an example", "write it for a 15-year-old".
  • Give an example. If you like a certain style, paste a short sample and say "write in a similar style".
  • Check facts. AI tools can make mistakes, especially with prices, dates and statistics. Always check important facts.
  • Do not paste private data. Avoid passwords, ID numbers and bank details in any AI chatbot.

Frequently asked questions

Is this ChatGPT prompt generator free?

Yes. It is completely free, with no sign-up and no limits. It runs in your browser, so you can use it as often as you like.

Does it work with Gemini and Claude too?

Yes. The prompt is plain text, so you can paste it into ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Claude, Microsoft Copilot, Meta AI or any other AI chatbot.

Does this tool use AI or save my text?

No. The tool simply arranges your answers into a well-structured prompt inside your browser. Your text is not saved or sent to our server.

What makes a good ChatGPT prompt?

A good prompt gives the AI a role, a clear task, the audience, useful background details and the format you want. It also tells the AI what to avoid and asks it to check with you if something is unclear.

Can I write the prompt in Urdu, Hindi or Arabic?

Yes. Type your task in any language, and set "Language of the answer" to the language you want the AI to reply in.

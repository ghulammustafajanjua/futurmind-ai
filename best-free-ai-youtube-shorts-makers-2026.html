Best Free AI YouTube Shorts Makers in 2026: 8 Tools Compared

Looking for an easier way to create YouTube Shorts? This beginner-friendly guide compares AI tools that can help with scripts, visuals, voiceovers, captions and short-form video editing.

YouTube Shorts · AI Video · Updated Aug 2026

AI tools can make parts of the YouTube Shorts workflow faster. Instead of manually creating every element, creators can use AI for ideas, scripts, generated visuals, voiceovers, subtitles and editing.

However, “free” does not always mean unlimited. Some platforms provide a free plan, while others offer limited AI credits or a free trial. Features and limits can change, so always check the provider before starting a large project.

1. CapCut — Best for Editing YouTube Shorts

CapCut is a practical choice for creators who already have video clips and want to turn them into polished vertical Shorts. It combines timeline editing with features such as captions, text, transitions, effects and other creator tools.

For beginners, CapCut can be especially useful as the final stage of an AI workflow. You can generate a script or visuals elsewhere and then assemble the finished Short in one editor.

Best for:

  • YouTube Shorts editing
  • Automatic captions
  • Vertical social videos
  • Combining AI-generated clips

2. Canva — Best for Templates and Visual Shorts

Canva is useful when your Short needs text, graphics, images, animations or branded visual elements. Its template-based workflow is easier for many beginners than a traditional professional editor.

It can also be useful for quote videos, educational Shorts, list-style content and simple faceless videos.

Best for:

  • Templates
  • Animated text
  • Thumbnails and graphics
  • Simple faceless content

3. InVideo AI — Best for Prompt-to-Video Workflows

InVideo AI is designed to reduce the amount of manual work required to build a video. A creator can start with an idea or prompt and use AI-assisted features as part of the creation process.

The platform currently provides limited free credits, which makes it possible to explore the service before deciding whether a paid plan is worthwhile.

Best for:

  • Prompt-based video creation
  • Beginners who want automation
  • Faceless video experiments
  • Testing AI-generated workflows

4. VEED — Best All-in-One Browser Video Tool

VEED combines online video editing with AI-powered creation features. Depending on the available plan and credits, creators can experiment with different AI video models and then continue editing in the same platform.

This can be convenient for creators who prefer browser-based tools instead of installing desktop editing software.

Best for:

  • Browser-based editing
  • AI video experiments
  • Captions and social videos
  • Creators who want several tools in one place

5. Pictory — Best for Turning Scripts into Videos

Pictory focuses heavily on turning written material into video. It includes script-to-video features, captions, stock media and AI voice options.

Pictory offers a free trial rather than a permanent unlimited free plan, so it is better described as a free-to-try option.

Best for:

  • Script-to-video
  • Faceless informational content
  • AI voiceovers
  • Repurposing written content

6. Kling AI — Best for Generating Creative Video Clips

Kling AI can generate video clips from prompts and images. It is particularly useful when a creator needs original AI-generated visual scenes rather than stock footage.

Availability, credits and generation limits can change, so check the current account limits before planning a full Shorts workflow around it.

Best for:

  • Text-to-video clips
  • Image-to-video
  • Creative AI scenes
  • Short visual sequences

7. Adobe Express — Best for Simple Social Video Design

Adobe Express provides beginner-friendly creation tools for social media graphics and video. It can be useful when a Short relies on text, images, branding and straightforward editing rather than complex AI-generated scenes.

Best for:

  • Social media videos
  • Text and graphics
  • Templates
  • Quick content creation

8. YouTube Create — Best Free Mobile Editing Option

YouTube Create is designed for creators who want to edit videos from a mobile device. For Shorts creators, keeping creation and publishing close to the YouTube ecosystem can make the workflow simpler.

Best for:

  • Mobile creators
  • Basic editing
  • YouTube-focused workflows
  • Beginners

Which AI YouTube Shorts Maker Is Best?

There is no single tool that is best for every creator. The right choice depends on which part of the process you want AI to handle.

  • For editing: CapCut
  • For visual templates: Canva
  • For prompt-based creation: InVideo AI
  • For browser-based AI editing: VEED
  • For script-to-video: Pictory
  • For generated visual clips: Kling AI
  • For simple design: Adobe Express
  • For mobile editing: YouTube Create

Simple AI Workflow for Creating a YouTube Short

  1. Choose one clear topic for the Short.
  2. Create a short hook and script.
  3. Generate or collect original visuals.
  4. Add a voiceover or record your own voice.
  5. Edit everything into a vertical video.
  6. Add readable captions.
  7. Review the video for mistakes and copyright issues.
  8. Upload the finished Short to YouTube.

YouTube currently allows square or vertical Shorts up to three minutes long, although many creators may choose a shorter format depending on the idea and audience.

Can You Make Faceless YouTube Shorts with AI?

Yes. A faceless workflow can combine a script, AI-generated or licensed visuals, narration, captions and editing without requiring the creator to appear on camera.

However, AI should be used as a production tool rather than a replacement for originality. Review generated content carefully, add your own creative decisions and make sure you have the necessary rights to everything you publish.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best free AI tool for making YouTube Shorts?

For editing existing clips, CapCut is a strong beginner option. Creators who want more automated generation can also test tools such as InVideo AI or VEED, subject to their current free-credit limits.

Can AI create an entire YouTube Short?

AI can assist with almost every stage, including ideas, scripts, visuals, narration, captions and editing. Human review is still important for quality, accuracy, originality and copyright checks.

Can I create YouTube Shorts without showing my face?

Yes. Faceless Shorts can use original graphics, screen recordings, licensed footage, animation, AI-generated visuals and voiceovers.

How long can a YouTube Short be in 2026?

YouTube supports square or vertical Shorts up to three minutes long for eligible uploads.

Are all AI YouTube Shorts makers completely free?

No. Some offer free features, while others use limited credits, trials or paid subscriptions. Always check the current plan before committing to a particular workflow.

Final Thoughts

The best AI YouTube Shorts maker depends on what you need. CapCut is useful for editing, Canva for visual design, InVideo AI for automated workflows, Pictory for script-based videos and Kling AI for generated visual clips.

Beginners do not need to use every tool at once. Start with one simple workflow, learn what produces the best results for your channel, and add other AI tools only when they solve a real problem.

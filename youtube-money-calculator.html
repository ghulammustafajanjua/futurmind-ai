How the YouTube money calculator works

YouTube does not pay a fixed amount per view. It shares the money from ads shown on your videos. The easiest way to estimate earnings is with RPM (revenue per mille), which means how much you earn for every 1,000 views after YouTube takes its share.

The formula is simple: earnings = views ÷ 1,000 × RPM. For example, 150,000 views a month with an RPM of $4 gives about $600. The calculator shows a low and a high estimate because RPM changes a lot from channel to channel.

What affects your RPM?

  • Niche. Advertisers pay more to reach people who are about to spend money. Finance, business and technology channels usually earn more per view than entertainment, gaming or music.
  • Viewer location. Views from the USA, UK, Canada and Australia usually pay much more than views from countries such as Pakistan or India. A Pakistani creator with a worldwide English audience can earn more than one with a local audience.
  • Video length. Videos longer than 8 minutes can show more ads in the middle, which can raise earnings.
  • Season. Advertisers spend the most from October to December and the least in January.
  • Shorts or long videos. Shorts earn far less per view, so many creators use them to get subscribers and long videos to earn.

When can you start earning?

To earn from ads, you must join the YouTube Partner Program (YPP). Until 31 January 2027, new channels need 1,000 subscribers and either 4,000 public watch hours in the last 12 months or 10 million Shorts views in the last 90 days.

YouTube has announced that from 1 February 2027, new applicants will need 1,000 subscribers and either 8,000 watch hours in 12 months or 20 million Shorts views in 90 days. Channels already in the program keep their status. Always check YouTube's own help pages for the latest rules.

Frequently asked questions

How much does YouTube pay for 1,000 views?

Most channels earn roughly $1 to $8 per 1,000 views on long videos, depending on the niche and where viewers live. Shorts usually earn only a few cents per 1,000 views.

How much does YouTube pay for 1 million views?

On long videos, often around $1,000 to $8,000, and more for finance and business channels. On Shorts, 1 million views may earn only about $10 to $70.

Is this calculator exact?

No calculator can be exact, because only YouTube knows your real ad rates. It gives a realistic range. Your real RPM is shown in YouTube Studio under Analytics, then Revenue.

What is the difference between RPM and CPM?

CPM is what advertisers pay for 1,000 ad views. RPM is what you actually earn per 1,000 video views after YouTube's share and counting views without ads. RPM is the better number for estimating your income.

Do I need to be in the YouTube Partner Program to earn?

Yes, to earn money from ads you must be accepted into the YouTube Partner Program. You can still earn from sponsorships and affiliate links before that.

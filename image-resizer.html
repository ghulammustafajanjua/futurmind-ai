How to resize an image for social media

  1. Choose your image. Click the box or drag a photo onto it. JPG, PNG and WebP all work.
  2. Pick a size, for example "YouTube thumbnail" or "Pinterest pin".
  3. Choose how to fit it. Fill crops the edges so the picture covers the whole frame. Fit keeps the whole picture and adds a coloured background around it.
  4. Choose the format and quality, then press Download image.

For a YouTube thumbnail, YouTube recommends 1280 × 720 pixels and a file under 2 MB. A JPG at 85 to 90% quality is usually well under that limit. For a channel banner, keep your text and logo in the centre, because TV, computer and phone screens show different parts of the image.

JPG, PNG or WebP?

  • JPG is best for photos and thumbnails. It makes small files that upload quickly.
  • PNG keeps sharp edges and text perfectly, and supports transparent backgrounds, but the files are bigger.
  • WebP makes the smallest files and is great for websites, but some older apps cannot open it.

Tips for better thumbnails and pins

  • Start with the biggest, sharpest image you have. Making a small image bigger always looks a little blurry.
  • Use Fill with the crop position set to Top when the face or main subject is near the top of the photo.

Frequently asked questions

Is this image resizer really free?

Yes. It is free with no limits, no sign-up and no watermark on your images.

Is my photo uploaded to a server?

No. The image is resized inside your own browser using your device. It is never uploaded, so it is safe for private photos.

What is the best size for a YouTube thumbnail?

1280 × 720 pixels (16:9) and under 2 MB. Choose the "YouTube thumbnail" size and the JPG format in this tool.

Why does my resized image look blurry?

The original image is smaller than the new size. Use a larger original image, or choose a smaller output size.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. It works in the browser on Android phones and iPhones. Tap the box to pick a photo from your gallery.

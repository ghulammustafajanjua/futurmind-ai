How to Make Faceless YouTube Shorts with AI for Free in 2026

A complete beginner workflow for creating faceless YouTube Shorts with AI for ideas, scripts, visuals, voiceovers, captions and editing.

Published: August 17, 2026 · By Ghulam Mustafa Janjua

You do not need to show your face to create useful YouTube Shorts. A faceless Short can use screen recordings, original graphics, AI-generated visuals, licensed footage, text animation or other visual formats combined with narration and captions.

AI can help with several parts of the workflow, but the final video should still feel original and useful. Simply generating large numbers of nearly identical videos is not a strong strategy.

The workflow below focuses on free and free-to-try tools so beginners can test the process before paying for subscriptions.

Quick Faceless Shorts Workflow

  1. Choose one clear niche or topic.
  2. Find one specific video idea.
  3. Write a short hook and script.
  4. Create or collect your visuals.
  5. Record or generate narration.
  6. Edit everything vertically.
  7. Add readable captions.
  8. Review AI disclosure requirements.
  9. Upload to YouTube Shorts.

Step 1: Choose a Faceless YouTube Shorts Niche

A faceless channel still needs a clear reason for viewers to watch. Choosing one focused niche makes it easier to create consistent videos and build an audience.

Beginner-friendly ideas

  • AI tools and tutorials
  • Technology tips
  • Interesting facts
  • Animal facts
  • Productivity tips
  • History facts
  • Simple educational explainers
  • Software tutorials

Avoid selecting a niche only because it appears easy to automate. Choose a topic where you can add useful information, storytelling or a distinct presentation style.

Step 2: Find One Clear Video Idea

Each Short should normally focus on one main idea instead of trying to explain an entire subject.

Examples:

  • One AI tool that saves time
  • Three useful ChatGPT prompts
  • One surprising animal fact
  • One CapCut editing trick
  • One productivity shortcut

Step 3: Write a Strong Hook

The first few seconds matter because viewers can immediately swipe to another Short.

Weak: “Today I am going to tell you about an AI tool.”

Better: “This free AI tool can turn your notes into a study guide in seconds.”

Step 4: Create the Script with AI

An AI writing assistant can help create a first draft, but you should review and improve the result before using it.

Example prompt:

Write a 30-second YouTube Short script about one useful AI tool. Start with a strong hook, use simple English, give one practical example, avoid filler and end with one useful takeaway.

Remove repeated phrases, check facts and rewrite anything that sounds generic or unnatural.

Step 5: Create Faceless Visuals

You have several options for creating visuals without appearing on camera.

  • Screen recordings
  • Your own smartphone footage
  • AI-generated images
  • AI-generated short clips
  • Original graphics
  • Text animation
  • Licensed stock footage

For AI video creation, short scenes are usually easier to control than asking one tool to generate an entire video at once.

For more detail, read: How to Create AI Videos from Text in 2026 .

Step 6: Add a Voiceover

You can use your own voice or a text-to-speech tool. A faceless video does not automatically require an AI voice.

If you prefer AI narration, compare tools in: Best Free AI Voice Generators in 2026 .

Voiceover tips

  • Use short sentences.
  • Check pronunciation before exporting.
  • Keep the narration synchronized with the visuals.
  • Avoid extremely fast speech.
  • Make sure you have the required usage rights.

Step 7: Edit the Short

Put the voiceover, visuals and text into a video editor. CapCut is one popular option for beginners, but you can use any editor that supports vertical video.

Example 30-second structure

  • 0–3 sec: Hook
  • 3–8 sec: Introduce the idea
  • 8–23 sec: Show the main value
  • 23–28 sec: Result or takeaway
  • 28–30 sec: Short call to action

Step 8: Add Captions

Captions help viewers follow the video when they are watching without sound.

  • Use readable text.
  • Keep captions short.
  • Do not cover important parts of the visual.
  • Check automatic captions for mistakes.

Step 9: Use Vertical or Square Format

YouTube currently categorizes eligible square or vertical videos up to three minutes as Shorts when they meet the platform's current requirements.

For typical mobile viewing, vertical video remains a practical choice for most Shorts creators.

Step 10: Upload and Review AI Disclosure

Before publishing, review the title, description, audience, copyright status, visibility and AI disclosure options.

YouTube requires disclosure when AI-generated or meaningfully altered content appears realistic in a way that could make viewers believe something happened when it did not.

Using AI only for ideas, scripts, captions or other minor production assistance does not automatically require the same disclosure.

Can Faceless AI Shorts Be Monetized?

AI itself does not automatically make a channel ineligible for monetization. The bigger issue is whether the channel provides original and authentic value.

YouTube's monetization policies specifically warn against repetitive or mass-produced content where videos feel interchangeable or follow generic templates with little value.

Focus on:

  • Original scripts
  • Useful information
  • Distinct storytelling
  • Meaningful editing
  • Different concepts between videos
  • Your own creative decisions

Free and Free-to-Try AI Tool Stack

Ideas & scripts: Use a general AI assistant.

Images: Use a free or free-to-try AI image tool.

Video clips: Test free credits from AI video platforms.

Voiceover: Use your own voice or a free AI voice option.

Editing: Use a beginner-friendly video editor such as CapCut.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Copying AI-generated scripts without reviewing them.
  • Using the same video template repeatedly.
  • Generating nearly identical videos at scale.
  • Using random visuals that do not match the narration.
  • Ignoring commercial-use rights.
  • Using copyrighted music without permission.
  • Skipping factual verification.
  • Failing to disclose realistic AI content when required.

Simple Example

Imagine you want to create a faceless Short titled: “3 AI Tools Every Student Should Try.”

  1. Use AI to brainstorm three relevant tools.
  2. Verify each tool's current features.
  3. Write a 30-second script.
  4. Record tool screens or create supporting visuals.
  5. Add your own or AI narration.
  6. Edit everything vertically.
  7. Add captions.
  8. Review the completed Short.
  9. Upload and complete YouTube's settings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I make faceless YouTube Shorts completely free?

You can start with free and free-to-try tools, but free plans often have limits on credits, exports, voices or advanced features.

Can I make Shorts without using my own voice?

Yes. You can use text-to-speech or AI voice tools, but always review licensing and commercial-use rights.

Can AI Shorts be monetized?

AI use alone does not automatically prevent monetization. Originality, value and compliance with YouTube's monetization policies are important.

How long can a YouTube Short be?

Eligible square or vertical videos can currently be categorized as Shorts when they are up to three minutes long.

Do I need to disclose AI content?

Disclosure is required when AI-generated or meaningfully altered content appears realistic and could mislead viewers about what actually happened.

Final Thoughts

Faceless YouTube Shorts can be a practical way for beginners to start creating content without appearing on camera.

Use AI to speed up repetitive work, but keep your videos useful, varied and original. The strongest long-term strategy is to build a recognizable style rather than generating large numbers of nearly identical Shorts.

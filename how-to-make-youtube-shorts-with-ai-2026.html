How to Make YouTube Shorts with AI in 2026

A simple step-by-step workflow for beginners to create YouTube Shorts using AI for ideas, scripts, visuals, voiceovers, captions and editing.

Published: August 10, 2026 · By Ghulam Mustafa Janjua

AI can make YouTube Shorts creation much faster, especially for beginners who do not have advanced video editing, scriptwriting or design skills.

You can use AI to generate ideas, write short scripts, create visual clips, make voiceovers, generate captions and organize your editing workflow.

YouTube currently accepts square or vertical Shorts up to three minutes long. For most beginner content, however, keeping the video focused and fast-paced is often easier.

Quick AI Shorts Workflow

  1. Choose one simple topic
  2. Write a strong opening hook
  3. Create a short script with AI
  4. Generate or collect visuals
  5. Add an AI or human voiceover
  6. Edit the clips together
  7. Add captions and sound
  8. Upload as a vertical YouTube Short

Step 1: Choose a Simple Topic

Do not start with a complicated story. One Short should usually focus on one main idea.

Examples:

  • 3 useful ChatGPT prompts for beginners
  • One surprising animal fact
  • One CapCut editing trick
  • One AI tool that saves time
  • A short before-and-after transformation

Step 2: Create a Strong Hook

The first few seconds are important because viewers can quickly swipe to another Short.

Instead of starting slowly, open with a clear reason to watch.

Weak: “Today I am going to show you an AI tool.”

Better: “This free AI tool can turn one sentence into a video.”

Step 3: Write the Script with AI

You can use an AI writing assistant to create the first version of your script.

Example prompt:

Write a 30-second YouTube Short script for beginners about one useful AI tool. Start with a strong hook, use simple English, give one practical example, and end with a short call to action. Avoid filler.

Always read the AI-generated script yourself. Remove repeated sentences and change anything that sounds unnatural.

Step 4: Create the Visuals

Your visuals can come from several sources depending on the type of Short you are making.

  • Your own smartphone footage
  • Screen recordings
  • AI-generated images
  • AI-generated short video clips
  • Simple text animation
  • Licensed stock footage

For an AI-generated Short, create short scenes instead of trying to generate the entire video in one prompt.

Step 5: Make a Voiceover

You can record your own voice or use a text-to-speech tool. A clear voice is more important than trying to sound overly dramatic.

Keep the narration synchronized with the visuals so viewers understand what they are seeing.

Step 6: Edit the Short

Put your generated clips, voiceover and text into your preferred editor.

A basic Short timeline might look like this:

  • 0–3 sec: Hook
  • 3–10 sec: Introduce the idea
  • 10–25 sec: Main value or demonstration
  • 25–30 sec: Result or conclusion

This is only an example. YouTube Shorts can be longer, but the story should remain focused.

Step 7: Add Captions

Captions make short videos easier to follow, especially for viewers who are watching without sound.

Keep captions readable. Avoid placing too much text on the screen at the same time.

Step 8: Use Vertical Format

YouTube recognizes uploaded square or vertical videos up to three minutes as Shorts when they meet the platform's current requirements.

For typical smartphone viewing, a vertical composition is usually the easiest format for Shorts creators.

Step 9: Upload Your Short

From YouTube Studio on a computer, choose Create → Upload videos and select your finished file.

Before publishing, review:

  • Title
  • Description
  • Audience setting
  • Copyright status
  • Altered or synthetic content disclosure when applicable
  • Visibility setting

Do You Need to Disclose AI Content?

YouTube requires disclosure when content is meaningfully altered or synthetically generated and appears realistic, such as a realistic scene that did not actually occur.

Simple AI assistance such as idea generation, script help, captions or minor production assistance does not automatically require the same disclosure.

If your Short contains realistic AI-generated footage, review the Altered content option during the YouTube upload process.

Can AI Shorts Be Monetized?

Using AI tools by itself does not automatically prevent monetization. What matters is whether your overall channel and content comply with YouTube's monetization and other platform policies.

Focus on providing your own value through useful information, original storytelling, commentary, editing or creative presentation instead of publishing large amounts of repetitive, low-value content.

Common Beginner Mistakes

  • Using a slow introduction
  • Putting too many ideas into one Short
  • Using AI output without checking mistakes
  • Uploading inconsistent AI characters between scenes
  • Using copyrighted music without permission
  • Adding too much text on screen
  • Publishing without watching the final video first

Simple Example Workflow

Imagine you want to make a Short called “3 AI Tools Every Beginner Should Try.”

  1. Ask an AI assistant for three tool ideas.
  2. Write a 30-second script.
  3. Record the tools on your screen or create supporting visuals.
  4. Record or generate the voiceover.
  5. Edit everything in a vertical timeline.
  6. Add large, readable captions.
  7. Watch the full Short before exporting.
  8. Upload it to YouTube and complete the required settings.

Final Tips

  • Make the first sentence interesting.
  • Show movement or visual change frequently.
  • Use AI as a helper, not as a replacement for judgment.
  • Test different topics and hooks.
  • Create a posting schedule you can realistically maintain.
  • Review YouTube policies as tools and platform rules change.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long can a YouTube Short be?

YouTube currently supports square or vertical Shorts up to three minutes long.

Can I make Shorts without showing my face?

Yes. You can use screen recordings, original graphics, animation, licensed footage, AI visuals and voiceovers.

Can ChatGPT write my Shorts script?

AI writing tools can create a useful first draft, but you should review and improve the script before publishing.

Do I need expensive software?

No. Beginners can start with free or free-to-try writing, video and editing tools, then upgrade only if needed.
